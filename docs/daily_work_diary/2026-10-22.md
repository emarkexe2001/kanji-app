## FEAT2_6 – Shuffled-out card view fixes (PRs #36, #39)
- A card with missing data only shows "This card can't be shown." in its own place, in both the Add back and Take out lists. The rest of the panel keeps working.
- In a one-card deck, Next and Deck Shuffle no longer remove the NEW ribbon.
- The deck is only saved again when something actually changed.
- Screen readers hear each pick card's meaning, its deck and "New".
- Holding Enter picks a card once.
- The NEW ribbon's colour comes from a shared token (`NEW_COLOR`) in `@kanji/core`.
- High-contrast (forced colours) mode, decided with a mockup and a browser check: a picked card turns fully yellow (Highlight) with black text and a ringed tick, and keyboard focus is a white dashed outline further out.

## ADJ2 – Floating Shuffled-out panel (PR #40)
Decided with a mockup (Option A):
- **Desktop (1024px and up):** the panel floats in the top right, just below the header. It is 300px wide with 2 cards per row, and its cards are slightly smaller. The cards scroll inside it, and Add Back Cards and Clear List are pinned at the bottom. The deck stays centred.
- **Phone and tablet (below 1024px):** the panel is a smaller pop-up, and while it is open the deck card shrinks and sits below it.
- **Closing:** with ×, Escape, a click outside, or the button again. Picks are dropped, as before.
- **Opening:** it slides down from the top right, or appears instantly with reduced motion on.
- **Review:** no must-fix items. The should-fix items became ticket ADJ2_5.

## Process lessons
- **PR #36 was merged before its follow-up fixes landed.** A merged PR can't be reopened, so the fixes went into a new PR (#39). From now on, a PR with fixes pending goes to draft with a "Do not merge yet" comment (standards 5.4).
- **Wait for the peer review before merging,** even after the browser check. Merge only if it has no must-fix items.
- **Mockups should check sizes against the deck column.** A 300px panel overlaps the deck controls on screens about 1024–1064px wide, which could have been spotted at the mockup stage.
- **Avoid hard-coded header heights** for placing things under the header.
- **When a requirement says "a card",** say which lists or views it covers.
- **The real-browser check finds things tests can't.** Here it found that picked and focused cards looked alike in high-contrast mode.
